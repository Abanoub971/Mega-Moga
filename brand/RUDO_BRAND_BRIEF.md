## 2. Why RUDO was chosen — DECIDED

### 2.1 The founder's taste profile (this drove the decision)
Found with a structured multiple-choice check after several rejected rounds:

- **Wanted feel:** like **Stink, Rogue, JOE**. Short, bold, with attitude, a "bad" word worn proudly.
- **Priority:** **how it sounds** first. Meaning is a bonus, not the point.
- **Language:** any language, as long as it sounds strong.
- **Rejected because:**
  - Soft or light sounds (e.g. Bennu felt too soft).
  - Names that need a history lesson to be understood (heritage words).
  - Safe, descriptive or cliché names.

### 2.2 How RUDO meets the brief
| Criterion | How RUDO meets it |
|---|---|
| Attitude | Spanish for "rough / tough". In Mexican wrestling, the *rudos* are the rule-breakers. In English it reads close to **rude**. Same family as Stink and Rogue |
| Sound | Two syllables. A hard **R** and a **D** stop. Back vowels **u / o** make it sound strong and heavy |
| Easy for everyone | No P or V (which Arabic speakers often turn into B / F). No ع ح خ ق غ ض ظ (hard for non-Arabic speakers). Same sound in Arabic and English |
| No bad meaning | No meaning in Egyptian, Gulf, Levantine or Maghrebi Arabic. No rude or negative hidden word found in English, French, German, Portuguese or Italian. Spanish meaning (rough) is on-brand |
| Story | A real, verifiable origin: Latin *rudis*, the Roman gladiator's wooden sword of freedom (Section 4) |

## 4. Brand story — DECIDED (core). Copy can be refined.

### 4.1 Core story: the Rudis, the wooden sword of freedom
In ancient Rome, a gladiator spent his life fighting in an arena that wasn't his, by rules that weren't his, for a crowd that wanted the same show every time. When one of them proved stronger than the arena itself, he was handed a simple, rough wooden sword called the **rudis**. It wasn't a weapon. It was **proof of freedom**. He never had to fight in someone else's arena again.

Today, brands are stuck in the arena: the same ad, the same framing, the same formula everyone copies. **RUDO is the moment a brand walks out of the arena.** We start raw, we break formula, and we make work that makes people stop.

### 4.2 Story — Arabic (Egyptian-friendly)
> زمان في روما، المصارع كان بيقضي عمره يحارب في حلبة مش بتاعته، وبقواعد مش بتاعته، قدام جمهور عايز نفس العرض كل مرة.
>
> ولما واحد فيهم يثبت إنه أقوى من الحلبة نفسها، كانوا بيدوهوله سيف خشب بسيط وخام اسمه **Rudis**. السيف ده مكانش سلاح، كان **إعلان حرية**.
>
> النهارده البراندات هي اللي في الحلبة: نفس الإعلان، ونفس الكادر، ونفس الفورمولا اللي الكل بيقلّدها.
>
> **Rudo** هي اللحظة اللي البراند بيطلع فيها من الحلبة. بنبدأ من الخام، وبنكسر القاعدة، وبنعمل الشغل اللي يخلي جمهورك يقف.

### 4.3 Short English version (pitch decks, website hero)
> In ancient Rome, a gladiator who outgrew the arena was handed a rough wooden sword called the *rudis*. It wasn't a weapon. It was proof of freedom.
> **Rudo exists to hand brands their rudis.** We start raw, we break formula, and we make work that walks out of the arena.

### 4.4 Optional human layer (use as one closing line only)
> In Shona, *Rudo* means **love**. Rough outside, love inside.
> بره خشنين، وجوه بنحب الشغل.

**Rule:** the gladiator / rudis story is the core. The Lucha Libre "rudos" idea adds attitude. The Shona "love" meaning is one optional line. Don't give all three equal weight in one piece of copy; it muddies the message.

### 4.5 One-sentence versions
- **EN:** Rudo is the production house that takes brands out of the formula arena, starting from raw.
- **AR:** رودو هي شركة الإنتاج اللي بتطلّع البراند من حلبة الفورمولا، وبتبدأ من الخام.

## 5. Brand symbol: "The Rudis Slash" ( / ) — PROPOSED, story-aligned, not yet designed

### 5.1 What it is
A **single diagonal stroke with raw, rough edges**, like a strike of wood or a dry brush. It sits beside the wordmark: **RUDO /**

### 5.2 What the slash means (four readings in one mark)
| The slash is… | Meaning for a production company |
|---|---|
| **The wooden sword (rudis)** | Freedom from formula |
| **The edit "cut"** | Editing: the cut that makes the film |
| **The clapperboard arm** | The first moment of every shoot |
| **A line that breaks a line** | Breaking the rule, the *rudos* attitude |

### 5.3 Usage ideas
| Where | How |
|---|---|
| Logo | `RUDO /`, with the slash as the standalone icon (avatar, favicon, stamp) |
| End-card signature | The slash cuts across the screen at the end of every video, then the logo appears. Same on every piece of work |
| Transitions | The slash used as a wipe in showreels and case studies |
| Client ritual | At project wrap, the client gets a **small wooden rudis** engraved with the project name and date. It's a physical keepsake that gets photographed and shared |
| Team ritual | Every new team member gets a rudis on their first delivered project |
| Merch / print | Wood-engraved or embossed slash. Stamp on call sheets and treatments |

### 5.4 Symbol rules
- ✅ Keep it **abstract**: one stroke.
- ❌ **Don't draw a realistic sword.** In this region weapon imagery can read as violent or militaristic.
- ❌ No Roman or gladiator clip-art, helmets or columns. The story is told in words; the mark stays minimal.
